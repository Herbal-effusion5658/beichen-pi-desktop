# Code of Conduct

Project participants must communicate respectfully and constructively.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, publication of private information, deliberate disruption, and knowingly sharing credentials or malicious payloads.

Technical disagreement is welcome; personal attacks are not. Maintainers may edit or remove abusive content and restrict participation when necessary.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers rather than escalating them in public threads.
